**The owner's new view:**

- The owner wants **private listings** in Turkey.

## Short answer

1. **The owner is right about businesses and visitors, but only half right about new residents.**
   - **New companies with an Iranian partner more than doubled:** 431 in Jan–Sep 2026, against 184 a year earlier (+134%). **74% of them are in Istanbul** (TOBB).
   - **Visitors recovered after the war shock.** Mar–Apr 2026 was −50%, but Jun–Aug was above 2025, so Jan–Aug ended only −1.7% (1.91M).
   - **The number of Iranian residents did not grow.**
     - Residence permits stand at **74,537** (15 Oct 2026), about the end-2025 level (74,213), after a peak of 76,124 in June.
     - About 62,000 more Iranians entered than left at the land border (28 Feb–18 Jun), but most were transit or circular trips.
   - **So Istanbul is a business-and-visitor market on top of a stable base of 75–92k residents.** It is not a growing resident market [assessment].
2. **Private listings in Turkey are legally possible in most categories, but not in housing or cars.**
   - **EİDS** (the e-Devlet ad-verification system) binds **any** platform that carries property or vehicle ads. It has no size threshold and no territorial limit in the text.
     - Real estate: Taşınmaz Ticareti Yönetmeliği Art. 12(2), amended in RG 31.08.2023.
     - Vehicles: Motorlu Kara Taşıtlarının Ticareti Yönetmeliği Art. 22, RG 27.08.2024.
   - **Rooms, roommates and sublets are worse than "needs EİDS"**:
     - Only the owner, close relatives or a licensed agent may post. Proxies are refused.
     - sahibinden itself refuses "ev/oda arkadaşı ve kiralık oda" ads "under the relevant legislation".
   - **Items, giveaways, lost and found, wanted posts (not housing), community events and courses by licensed schools have no EİDS-like rule.**
     - Law 5651, KVKK and a prohibited-items list (phones without IMEI registration, cats and dogs for sale, medicines, alcohol and tobacco, and others) are what apply.
     - An ad-only site is outside ETBİS (the e-commerce registry) and the Law 6563/7416 intermediary thresholds.
   - **Jobs are possible later**, if we follow İŞKUR's rule for job-ad sites: no apply button, no CV database, and contact details open to everyone.
3. **Phased plan (§5):**
   - **Feb 2027:** verified businesses and organisers in Istanbul.
   - **1 Mar 2027:** the Nowruz events page, with organiser events, before Nowruz on 20 Mar.
   - **May 2027:** private listings in safe categories, in time for students selling up in June.
   - **Sep 2027:** jobs from verified businesses, private lessons and services, and more cities.
   - **Never on Peydoo:** property (rooms included) and cars.
   - **Preconditions:** KVKK (a representative and VERBIS) and a short Turkish legal opinion, both **before the first Turkish account**, which now means before February, not before the first payment.
4. **"They can lie, so what is the guarantee?"**
   - **There is no guarantee, and the law doesn't ask for one.**
     - EU Reg. 267/2012 Art. 42(2) and Reg. 2023/1529 Art. 10(2) remove liability if we "did not know, and had no reasonable cause to suspect".
     - In Germany the realistic exposure is the **negligence** fine (§19 AWG, up to €500k), not the crime (§18, intent only).
   - **The defence is a written, consistent process built on facts the business does not write itself:**
     - the public trade-registry gazette (TTSG), which is free to search after a free sign-up;
     - barcode checks on e-Devlet and ITO (the Istanbul Chamber of Commerce);
     - screening of every partner and manager, with corporate partners looked through to people;
     - payment only from the company's own Turkish bank account, so that bank's know-your-customer checks also apply;
     - a signed ownership declaration;
     - records kept for 5 years after the relationship ends.
   - **What nobody can catch:** a professional nominee, and the current shareholders of an A.Ş. (a Turkish joint-stock company), which are not public.
   - **Our answers:** forbidden sectors, Ltd. Şti. (limited companies) first, A.Ş. only with their share register shown live, and red flags.
5. **Verification can cost us about €10–15 per business instead of €20–35.**
   - Replace the default video call with three things: a WhatsApp callback to the company's public number, payment from the company account, and the TTSG notice checked by us.
   - Accept Ltd. Şti. first, and treat A.Ş. as the slower case.
   - The automated screening costs **€0.50–1 per business**.
   - Paid know-your-business (KYB) services don't help yet. Didit's Turkey shareholder lookup ($5–9) is still "staging". The big vendors only quote on request.
   - **Use the margin to include more** in the €49: a basic profile, 2 featured events and Nowruz/Yalda guide placement. A €29 Ltd-only tier is also possible.
6. **KVKK is cheaper than feared:**
   - Prighter's Türkiye representative costs **€420–852 a year plus a €2,000 one-off setup** [1 source].
   - **Year 1 ≈ €2.4–2.9k, then about €0.4–0.9k a year.**

## 2. Private listings in Turkey: what is legally possible

### 2.1 EİDS: the exact rule

**Real estate: Taşınmaz Ticareti Hakkında Yönetmelik**
- Original: RG 05.06.2018 / 30442 ([Lexpera, consolidated text](https://www.lexpera.com.tr/mevzuat/yonetmelikler/tasinmaz-ticareti-hakkinda-yonetmelik)).
- **Art. 12(2)** was rewritten by **RG 31.08.2023 / 32295** ([Resmî Gazete](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2023/08/20230831-6.htm)), in force about 31 Oct 2023.
- It binds "**natural or legal persons providing an electronic environment for ads for the sale of property belonging to others**". Before membership or publication, the platform must:
  - (c) verify the poster's name, T.C. **or foreign identity number** (*yabancı kimlik numarası*) and phone;
  - **(ç) verify that the property belongs to the poster, or to their 1st/2nd-degree blood relatives or spouse, or that the owner has authorised the poster**;
  - also: complaint channels, data to the Ministry on request, and measures against misleading ads.
- **Later amendments:** RG 30.05.2025 (Art. 12(3), no unjustified price increases); RG 29.04.2026 (scope and licences; content not fully checked); RG 01.10.2026 (secure payment system).

**Rentals are in practice, though the text says "sale":**
- The Ministry says "EİDS authority verification will be mandatory for both sale and rental property ads" ([Afyon Ticaret İl Müdürlüğü FAQ](https://afyon.ticaret.gov.tr/haberler/elektronik-ilan-dogrulama-sistemi-eids-nedir-nasil-kullanilir), checked again by me today).
- No amendment adding "rental" was found. That wording gap is a question for the lawyer, not a loophole to build on [assessment].

**Real-estate timeline:**

| Date | Step | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ~1 Nov 2023 | Identity verification | Ministry |
| 15 Sep 2024 | Authority verification launched | [Ministry, 17 Sep 2024](https://ticaret.gov.tr/kurumsal-haberler/elektronik-ilan-dogrulama-sistemi-eids-yetki-dogrulama-uygulamasi-hayata-gecirildi) |
| **1 Jan 2025** | Mandatory for rental ads | [Acar Ergönen](https://acarergonen.av.tr/tr/yayinlar/duyurular/tasinmaz-ilanlarinda-elektronik-kimlik-ve-yetki-dogrulamasina-iliskin-bilgi-notu) |
| Feb 2026 | Sale ads | Pilot 1 Feb, nationwide 15 Feb; the sources disagree |

**Vehicles: Motorlu Kara Taşıtlarının Ticareti Hakkında Yönetmelik**
- RG 27.08.2024 / 32645 ([Resmî Gazete](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2024/08/20240827-1.htm)), which replaced the 2018 second-hand vehicle regulation.
- Art. 4(g) defines the "ad platform" (*ilan platformu*).
- **Art. 22:**
  - verify identity and phone before membership or publication, **and at least once a calendar year**;
  - keep records for 5 years;
  - verify ownership or authorisation through the Ministry's systems before publishing.
- **Mandatory for all vehicle ads since 16 Jun 2025** ([Ministry, 18 Jun 2025](https://ticaret.gov.tr/haberler/ticaret-bakanligindan-yeni-ve-zorunlu-uygulama-tum-tasit-ilanlarinda-eids-yetki-dogrulama-uygulamasi-basladi)).
- It covers sale and marketing ads. Vehicle *rental* ads look outside it [assessment].

**Who is bound, and the penalty:**
- **Any** natural or legal person running the ad environment.
- **No size threshold. No territorial limit in the text.** Law 6563 has no explicit clause on foreign providers.
- The Ministry announced on **22 Aug 2026** that EİDS covers "all property and vehicle ads given electronically, including Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp". It said 82 businesses were fined TRY 100k each and Meta was asked to close the accounts ([Ministry](https://ticaret.gov.tr/haberler/ticaret-bakanligi-tasinmaz-ve-tasit-ticaretinde-uygulanmakta-olan-elektronik-ilan-dogrulama-sistemi-eids-kapsaminda-kimlik-ve-yetki-dogrulamasi-zorunlulugunu-instagram-facebook-ve-whatsapp-da-dahil-olmak-uzere-elektronik-ortamda-verilen-tum-tasinmaz-ve-tasi)).
- **Platform penalty:** Law 6563 Art. 12(1)(d), **TRY 10k–100k per violation** (base). That is about **TRY 28.6k–286k in 2026** (≈€510–5,140) **[1 source]**. It doubles for repeats.
- **The realistic enforcement against a foreign site is access blocking** [assessment]. That would hit every Peydoo page in Turkey.
- **Technically**, verification runs "automatically" through the platform's integration with EİDS. The private owner enters the property number (*taşınmaz numarası*).
- **I found no public API or procedure for a foreign platform to integrate.**

**Does it cover the categories Peydoo cares about?**

| Case | Covered? | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Private owner selling or renting their own flat | **Yes**. Possible only through an EİDS-integrated platform. | Ministry 2024; FAQ |
| **A tenant subletting a room, roommate ads, "room for rent"** | **Effectively banned.** The tenant is not the owner or a relative. Owners can authorise only licensed agents. "Because authorisation is personal, no ad can be given by proxy." sahibinden refuses "ev/oda arkadaşı ve kiralık oda" ads "under the relevant legislation", and also "ads seeking shared living". | [Afyon FAQ](https://afyon.ticaret.gov.tr/haberler/elektronik-ilan-dogrulama-sistemi-eids-nedir-nasil-kullanilir); sahibinden help pages (search summaries only; the pages return 403) **[1 source]** |
| Daily or short-term rentals under 100 days | EİDS, plus **Law 7464**: a permit is needed for every home rented to tourists. A platform that doesn't remove an ad within **24 h** of a Ministry warning is fined **TRY 100k per dwelling** (about TRY 180k in 2026, **[1 source]**). Platforms report monthly under Genelge 2024/3. | [Law 7464](https://www.lexpera.com.tr/mevzuat/kanunlar/konutlarin-turizm-amacli-kiralanmasina-ve-bazi-kanunlarda-degisiklik-yapilmasina-dair-kanun-7464); [Genelge 2024/3](https://www.alomaliye.com/2024/06/20/konutlarin-turizm-amacli-kiralanmasina-iliskin-2024-3-genelge/) |

| Property without a land title ("Tapum Yok") | Outside the system | Afyon FAQ |
| Business profile of an agency, with no units | Outside, as in round 6 [assessment] | — |

**Conclusion for Peydoo:**
- **Housing of any kind (rooms, roommates, sublets, daily rentals) and cars stay closed in Turkey.** "Wanted housing" also stays closed.
- This holds until there is a Turkish entity with EİDS integration. That would be a different business.
- The one open lawyer question: may a *verified agency* link to its EİDS-verified ad on a Turkish portal? That follows the Aug 2026 social-media rule. It is for later at the earliest.

### 2.2 Categories with no EİDS-like rule

**Rules across all categories:**

| Rule | Applies to Peydoo? | What we must do |
|---|---|---|
| **Law 6563** (e-commerce), as amended by **Law 7416** (2022) ([text](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.6563.doc)) | Peydoo is probably an "intermediary service provider" (*aracı hizmet sağlayıcı*) in the broad sense. **It is probably not an e-commerce marketplace intermediary (ETAHS)**, because no contract or order is made on the site [assessment]. ### 2.3 Phone verification for Turkish numbers

- **Route:** the round 8 ladder, step 2. **The user starts a WhatsApp chat** to our number with a token (a wa.me link). Incoming messages are free.

- **SMS stays closed in Turkey.** I could not check the operators' sender-ID registration rules this round.
- **Rule for Turkey private accounts:** only **+90 numbers** may post private listings in Turkey.
  - +98 is refused as everywhere.
  - Other foreign numbers can browse and message, but not post in Turkey.
- **Why +90 helps:** Turkish operators register SIM cards to an ID. A +90 number therefore ties the account to an identity Turkish authorities can trace. That deters drive-by scammers [assessment; I did not check the BTK rule text].

## 3. Sanctions screening: "they can lie, so what is the guarantee?"

### 3.1 The honest answer

**There is no guarantee. No platform, bank or KYB vendor has one. The law knows that and asks for something else.**

| Text | What it says | Source |
|---|---|---|

**[assessment]** If a business lies to us and we followed a sensible process, we did not know and had no reasonable cause to suspect. The realistic risk is a **negligence** finding: we ignored a red flag, skipped a step, or kept no record. **The process and the records are the guarantee we can offer.**

### 3.2 "Owned or controlled": the 50% rules

- **EU Best Practices** ([Council doc 11623/24, 3 Jul 2024](https://data.consilium.europa.eu/doc/document/ST-11623-2024-INIT/en/pdf)):
  - **¶63, ownership:** 50% or more of the proprietary rights. Holdings of different listed persons are **added together**.
  - **¶64, control:** for example, appointing a majority of the board, dominant influence by contract, or de facto influence "including … by means of a front company".
  - **¶67, red flags:** the listed person is the largest shareholder (40% against holdings of 10%), or "transfer of shares at a time close to the designation".
  - **¶68:** funds given to an owned or controlled company count as indirect making available, unless it is "reasonably determined … using a risk-based approach".
- **CJEU C-84/24 (12 Mar 2026):** 50% creates a *rebuttable* presumption of control, and de facto influence can be enough. Cited in the [Bundesbank FAQ](https://www.bundesbank.de/resource/blob/886614/4355fc5861c5d6817567b440e737fad1/472B63F073F071307366337C94F8C870/faq-finanzsanktionen-data.pdf) **[1 source]**.
- **OFAC:**
  - **The 50% rule** ([guidance, Aug 2014](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/6186/download?inline); [FAQs 398–402](https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/topic/1521)): aggregate direct and indirect ownership of 50% or more by blocked persons. The rule covers ownership only, but OFAC warns about control and stakes below 50%.
  - **31 Mar 2026 advisory on sham transactions** ([OFAC](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/935441/download)): treat 50% as a floor, and look at economic reality.
  - **The [Framework for Compliance Commitments](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/16331/download)** (2019): programmes are risk-based and depend on size, products, customers and geography.
- **Peydoo is a non-US person.**
  - US civil liability is strict only for US persons.
  - Our real US exposure is secondary sanctions, which target "significant" transactions. A €49 listing is very unlikely to count [assessment].
  - **The practical exposure is losing Stripe or the bank, and reputation.**
- **Turkish companies OFAC listed in 2026, as examples of the sectors to forbid:**
  - Amon Kimya, Diako Dış Tic., Starex Dış Tic. Kimya ([6 Feb](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20260206));
  - Huzur Plastik, Oshida Petrokimya, Selfplast, Starplas Kimya ([24 Aug](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20260824));
  - Golden Global Yatırım Bankası ([4 Sep](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20260904)).
- **EU Blocking Statute:** the 1996 Iran–Libya act and the other US laws it lists are in its annex, but EO 13846 and EO 13902 are not.
  - **Rule:** refuse an OFAC-only hit under **Peydoo's own written risk policy** (payment-provider, bank and reputational risk), not "because OFAC requires it" [assessment; for the sanctions lawyer].

### 3.4 What lies can and can't beat

| The lie | Our control that doesn't rely on their word | Beaten? |
|---|---|---|
| A false company name or VKN | Barcode check on e-Devlet; TTSG lookup by us | Caught |
| False partner names | We read the partners from the TTSG ourselves (Ltd) | Caught for Ltd |
| A hidden listed partner in an A.Ş. | The share register or attendance list shown live, plus a signed declaration | Partly. A forged register is possible, but then the fault is theirs. |
| A corporate partner that hides the owner | Look through to natural persons, or refuse | Caught, or refused |
| **A Turkish nominee in front of a listed Iranian** | Red flags: share transfers in the last 12 months (especially from an Iranian to a new partner), and a forbidden sector | **Not reliably caught by anyone** [assessment]. Banks and governments miss these too. |
| A fake applicant ("I am the manager") | A WhatsApp callback to the **public** company number (from the website or Google Maps); payment from the company's own Turkish account; optionally a video call matching an ID against the registry name | Mostly caught |
| A listed person paying for an unlisted company | Payment only from the company's own account; no third-party payers | Caught at payment |
| The status changes after approval | Daily diff against the new list entries; re-check on any change of partner or manager; yearly renewal | Caught within a day |

**The forbidden categories carry most of the weight.** The 2026 designations were chemicals, plastics, shipping, banking, FX and gold. **None was a restaurant, salon or language school** [assessment from the OFAC action pages]. Keeping Turkey to consumer-facing categories removes most of the real risk before any name check.

### 3.5 The defensible minimum (write it down as "Peydoo sanctions policy, Turkey")

1. **Scope:**
   - the company, every partner at any percentage, all managers, board members and signatories;
   - corporate partners **looked through to people**;
   - holdings added together, with 50% or more treated as presumed owned.
2. **Lists:** EU (all regimes, not only Iran), UN, UK, OFAC SDN and non-SDN. All are free XML:
   - [EU FSF](https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/fsd/fsf/public/files/xmlFullSanctionsList_1_1/content?token=dG9rZW4tMjAxNw)
   - [OFAC](https://sanctionslistservice.ofac.treas.gov/api/PublicationPreview/exports/SDN.XML) (use SDN_ADVANCED for Arabic-script names)
   - [UN](https://scsanctions.un.org/resources/xml/en/consolidated.xml)
   - [UK](https://sanctionslist.fcdo.gov.uk/docs/UK-Sanctions-List.xml)

   The EU file has 302 Iran entries with Arabic-script aliases. The UN and UK files carry non-Latin names too. Copies are in `scratchpad/lists/`.
3. **Ownership from a public record** (TTSG for Ltd). For an A.Ş.: the share register or attendance list shown live, plus a signed declaration. **Treat an A.Ş. with more than 5 shareholders, or a corporate shareholder that won't disclose, as refused** [assessment].
4. **Red flags lead to manual review:** share transfers within 12 months; a forbidden sector; an address shared with listed companies; a sanctions-adjacent trade name (*petro*, *kimya*, *dış ticaret*, *lojistik*, *döviz*, *altın*).
5. **Payment:** only from the company's own account or card, never from a third party.
6. **A signed declaration:**
   - no listed person owns 25% or more or controls the company;
   - the partners listed are complete;
   - changes will be reported within 30 days;
   - a false statement ends the listing without refund.
7. **Escalation:** unclear cases go to the free [EU Sanctions Helpdesk](https://eu-sanctions-compliance-helpdesk.europa.eu/). A true hit means freeze, report to the Bundesbank (funds) or BAFA (economic resources), and call the lawyer. Never tell the business why ("under review").
8. **Records (facts only, no ID copies):**
   - the date and who checked;
   - the names screened;
   - the list versions or hashes;
   - the TTSG issue number, date and page;
   - the candidates and their scores;
   - the decision and its reason;
   - the declaration;
   - a one-line note of the call.

   **Keep them for 5 years after the relationship ends.** That matches the 5-year limitation for §18 AWG; for §19 fines it is 3 years.

**Private users' listings are not screened** [assessment].
- Their names aren't verified, so screening them would mean nothing.
- In Turkey they pay nothing, because nothing is sold to Turkish consumers (§4.3).
- Screening applies to anyone who pays, to business accounts, and to verified organisers.

### 3.6 Making verification cheaper

**Mostly automated, with a short human review:**
1. The business fills the form: VKN, MERSİS, trade name, partners and managers, category, and the barcode of its tax plate and *faaliyet belgesi*. The category picker rejects forbidden sectors.
2. **Automatic:**
   - name screening through the OpenSanctions API (€0.10 per name; about 5–10 names makes **€0.50–1**);
   - normalisation before matching:
     - Arabic to Persian letters (ي→ی, ك→ک), with ZWNJ and diacritics stripped;
     - Turkish letters folded (ı ş ç ğ ü ö);
     - variant tables (Mohammad, Muhammed, Mehmet; Hossein, Hüseyin; Reza, Rıza; -zadeh, -zade);
     - ICU transliteration of the Persian-script aliases;
   - then the daily local diff of the four free lists with `pg_trgm` (round 7);
   - the open-source helpers: [rigour](https://github.com/opensanctions/rigour) (MIT) and [Moov Watchman](https://github.com/moov-io/watchman) (Apache-2.0).
   - OpenSanctions' bulk data needs a paid licence for in-house screening, even when that screening earns nothing ([OpenSanctions](https://www.opensanctions.org/docs/commercial/exemption/)). **So use the API for each sign-up and the official lists for the daily diff.**
3. **Human, the owner:**
   - two barcode checks (captchas stop automation): 4 min;
   - the TTSG lookup and reading the partners (Ltd): 8–12 min;
   - clearing any screening hits: 0–5 min;
   - a **WhatsApp callback to the public company number**: 3 min.
4. **The money arrives from the company's own account.** Bank transfer or a company card; the payer name must match.
5. **A video call only on a red flag, or for an A.Ş.** (10–15 min).
