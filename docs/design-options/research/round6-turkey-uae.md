# Peydoo: an "events + businesses only" mode for Turkey and the UAE (research 6)

25 Oct 2026 · web research and repo docs only, nothing in the app changed. This builds on `docs/design-options/research/round5-countries.md` (§4 Turkey, §5 UAE), `round5-iran.md` and `docs/payments-iran.md`.

> **This is not legal or tax advice.** Every legal, sanctions and VAT point is background for the lawyer and the tax advisor. **[1 source]** marks a fact I found in only one source. "My estimate" marks numbers I made up from reasoning, not from a source.

**The owner's idea:** "For countries like Turkey and the UAE, events and businesses would be a very good option, because there are many businesses from Iran doing work there."

## Short answer

| | Turkey | UAE |
|---|---|---|
| **Verdict** | **Go, with conditions.** Events + verified, consumer-facing businesses. No person-to-person (P2P) listings and no payments at first. | **No-go for now.** Keep it hidden. Review in Q4 2027 at the earliest, and only if the conditions in §5 are met. |
| **When** | Now: the guide and waitlist from round 5, plus "I'm a business / organiser" sign-ups. Nowruz 2027 (20 Mar): an editorial events page as a demand test. **Months 6–9 (mid-2027): starter in Istanbul**, if the lawyer has signed off. | Not before Q4 2027. If it ever opens, start with **events only** (each with a DET permit number). Businesses come later or never. |
| **Main blockers** | KVKK (VERBIS plus a representative), Law 5651 blocking, Turkish rules that restrict advertising by lawyers and doctors, sanctions screening, verification work | War and a trade halt with Iran (18 Aug 2026), Iranian concerts cancelled, the media law (licences for platforms that carry advertising), health-advertising licences, VAT from the first dirham for consumer sales, the main hub for sanctions evasion |

**Why "events + businesses" fits Turkey better than the UAE:**
- In Turkey, EİDS (the e-Devlet ad-verification system) only covers property and vehicle ads. Business profiles and events fall outside it.
- In the UAE, by contrast, the media law treats any website that carries advertising "for or without compensation" as needing a licence. A business directory is advertising by that definition.

**Correcting the premise (honest version):**
- **"Many businesses from Iran" is true**: Turkey has 7,288 to about 12,000 companies with Iranian partners, and the UAE has historically had 8,000 to 10,000 Iranian-owned firms.
- **But most of them are traders, import-export houses, logistics firms and money changers.** These are exactly the categories we have to forbid.
- **The consumer-facing part is far smaller.** Restaurants, salons, clinics, lawyers, translators and schools probably come to a few hundred in Istanbul (my estimate).

## 1. Demand

### 1.1 Turkey

**Events**
- **Tourists are the main audience.**
  - More than **3 million** Iranians visited Turkey in 2025 ([TCCIM](https://en.tccim.ir/story?nid=2466)) **[1 source]**.
  - Arrivals fell **23.3% to about 904,000 in Jan–May 2026** because of the war ([Hürriyet Daily News](https://hurriyetdailynews.com/amp/foreign-tourist-arrivals-to-turkiye-decline-2-6-percent-in-january-may-223554)).
  - Iran was still the top source country in Jan 2026, with 225k visitors ([Forbes TR](https://www.forbes.com.tr/ekonomi/yabanci-ziyaretci-sayisi-ocakta-artti)).
- **Van** gets roughly 700–780k Iranian entries a year. Local figures disagree on the year ([search summary of Van press](https://www.haberturk.com/van-haberleri/115927045-vana-yilbasi-icin-iranli-akini)) **[1 source]**.
  - New Year (31 Dec) and Nowruz are the peaks. Hotels hold their own parties, and the 2026 New Year was "somewhat lower" than before ([24saat](https://www.24saatgazetesi.com/iranli-turistler-yilbasina-vanda-girdi)) **[1 source]**.
  - Older reports show Moein concerts in Van for tourists ([CNN Türk](https://www.cnnturk.com/turkiye/vana-akin-ettiler), undated).
- **Concerts by Persian artists exist, but they are irregular, and the state cancels some of them.**
  - Recent examples: Alireza Ghorbani at AKM, the state-run cultural centre ([AKM](https://akmistanbul.gov.tr/event/alireza-ghorbani-bkyf)), and a Namjoo tour that included Volkswagen Arena.
  - But the Çankaya district governor **refused permission for Mohsen Namjoo's Ankara concert on 13 June 2026**, citing "public sensitivities" ([Evrensel](https://www.evrensel.net/haber/5986964/iranli-sarkici-mohsen-namjoo-nun-ankara-konseri-iptal-edildi), [Gazete Oksijen](https://gazeteoksijen.com/muzik/ankarada-sahne-alacakti-iranli-sarkici-mohsen-namjoonun-konseri-bir-kez-daha-yasaklandi-277871)). His 2022 Istanbul dates were cancelled too ([Bianet](https://bianet.org/haber/mohsen-namjoo-nun-istanbul-konseri-de-iptal-268721)).
- **Political events are blocked.** The Fatih district governor banned a press statement by Iranians outside the Istanbul consulate on 11 Jan 2026 ([Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/01/turkey-bars-iranians-protesting-outside-istanbul-consulate), [Hürriyet DN](https://hurriyetdailynews.com/istanbul-bans-protests-around-iranian-consulate-217718)).
- **Ticketing:** Biletix, Passo, Biletinial and Bubilet are the Turkish systems, and promoters reach Iranians through Instagram and Telegram.
  - **I found no source** on how many Persian events use which system, or on promoters' audience sizes (weak evidence).
- **Residents:** 134k people born in Iran, 74–81k residence permits and 31.5k students (round 5, §2). This is the community that would hold Yalda, Nowruz and student events.

**Businesses**
- **Companies with Iranian partners:**
  - **7,288 operating companies with Iranian investment**, 2,040 of them registered with the Istanbul Chamber of Commerce (an ITO representative quoted by [TCCIM](https://en.tccim.ir/story?nid=2466), 2025) **[1 source]**;
  - about **12,000 with an Iranian partner, 8,693 of them in Istanbul**, as of June 2025 ([Ekonomi Gazetesi column citing TEPAV](https://www.ekonomigazetesi.com/kose-yazisi/turkiyede-iranlilarin-kurdugu-12-bin-turk-sirketi-var-73838)) **[1 source]**;
  - after Istanbul come Antalya, Ankara, İzmir and Van.
- **New companies:** over 3,000 a year were founded in 2022–23 (same column). TOBB's monthly figures for 2026 show **41 (April), 52 (May) and 56 (August)** a month ([QNB/TOBB](https://www.qnbinvest.com.tr/investodak/qnbarastirma/temmuz-2026da-turkiyede-kurulan-ve-kapanan-sirket-sayilari-belli-oldu), [TOBB May 2026](https://www.tobb.org.tr/BilgiErisimMudurlugu/Documents/ResmiDosya/2026/2026-05.docx), [Foreks](https://www.foreks.com/haber/detay/6aace2c924008d7d437337e1/FRKS/tr/yenileme-yilin-ilk-sekiz-ayinda-kurulan-sirket-sayisi-gecen-yilin-ayni-donemine-gore-yuzde-6-4-artti-18-09-26/)). Dec 2025 had 21 ([Capital](https://www.capital.com.tr/haberler/tum-haberler/gecen-yil-kurulan-sirket-sayisi-yuzde-15-azaldi)).
  - **So the wave has slowed to about 500–650 a year (my extrapolation).**
- **Sectors:** no breakdown found. Press and the trade figures ($5.6bn of Iran–Turkey trade in 2025) point mainly to **trade, logistics and real estate**.
  - The consumer-facing services an Iranian would look for are a minority: restaurants, bakeries and groceries, salons, translators, lawyers, accountants, clinics (hair and dental tourism), language and Konkur schools, *ikamet* and education agents.

## 2. Risks specific to this mode

### 2.1 Sanctions (both countries)

**Exposure is real and recurring.** In 2026 OFAC designated:
- three Istanbul firms financing the Shahed-engine maker (25 Feb; [Turkish Minute](https://turkishminute.com/2026/02/26/us-sanctions-3-turkey-based-companies-tied-to-production-of-irans-shahed-drones/amp/));
- oil and weapons networks in Turkey and the UAE (20 Mar; [Thomson Reuters](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en-us/help/onesource-global-trade/regulatory-insights/2026/march-20th/us-imposes-sanctions-iran-shadow-fleet-weapons));
- weapons procurement in Iran, Turkey and the UAE (21 Apr);
- arms networks including the UAE (8 May);
- LPG smuggling via Turkey and the UAE (5 Jun) ([Akin](https://www.akingump.com/en/insights/blogs/overruled-sanctions-export-controls-update/overruled-updates-09-Jun-2026)).

The **EU** lists sit in Reg. 267/2012 (restored by the snapback), Reg. 2023/1529 (Iran drones and missiles) and Reg. 359/2011 (human rights). The **UN** 1737 list is back since 28 Sep 2025 (`payments-iran.md`).

**Why this matters even for a free directory:**
- A business page with Pro features may be "economic resources" for a listed person. That question is already open with the lawyer (round5-iran Q5).
- **OFAC's 50% rule** (entities owned 50% or more by people on the list are treated as listed) can't be caught by checking names alone. We need to see who owns each company.
- One listed company on Peydoo would also put the Stripe account at risk.

**Screening for every business account** (company name, every owner or partner named on the registry extract, every manager or staff member on the account):

| Option | Cost | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Manual: [EU Financial Sanctions Files](https://ec.europa.eu/external_relations/cfsp/sanctions/list/version4/global/help_online/process_xmlfiles.html) (free XML, daily delta), OFAC Sanctions List Search (free), UN list (free XML), Germany's finanz-sanktionsliste.de | €0, about 10 min per business | Feasible for the first 30–50 businesses. Weak on spelling variants between Persian, Turkish and Latin script. The EU sanctions map is an overview, not a screening tool. |
| Our own matching on the free official lists | €0, a few days to build | Fuzzy matching across Persian, Turkish and Arabic spellings is the hard part. That's a real risk of missing someone. |
| **[OpenSanctions API](https://www.opensanctions.org/api/)** | **€0.10 per call** (500 for €50, credits valid 1 year); **2,000 free trial credits** with a business email | **Recommended.** 200 businesses × (onboarding + 12 monthly re-checks) × about 2 names ≈ 5,000 calls ≈ €400 a year. The bulk data is CC BY-NC, so screening from it on our own server for a business needs a paid licence ([licensing](https://www.opensanctions.org/licensing/)); the API is the cheap route. |

**Rules:**
- **Screen at sign-up, on every change of owner or manager, and monthly.**
- **Any possible match:** suspend the page and review it by hand.
- **Record the result** in `audit_log`.
- **Never** tell the business that it matched a sanctions list; say "under review".

**Categories to forbid outright** in TR/AE (and probably everywhere):
- currency exchange, *sarrafi* and *havaleh*;
- payment agents and "Iranian card top-ups";
- import-export or trading with Iran;
- freight, cargo and shipping to or from Iran;
- oil, gas, petrochemicals and bunkering;
- electronics, industrial parts, machinery, aviation and drone parts (dual-use goods);
- gold and precious metals;
- crypto exchanges, crypto OTC desks and USDT sellers;
- **company formation, bank-account opening and "nominee" services**, the classic way to set up front companies;
- visa, *ikamet*, citizenship and golden-visa "fixers";
- Schengen appointment sellers;
- university placement agents (the Turkish scam pattern, round 5 §4);
- recruitment and employment agencies;
- people-moving or "border" services;
- in Turkey at the start: **real estate agencies and car dealers** (EİDS, the property-for-citizenship anti-money-laundering risk, and scams).

### 2.2 Payments and VAT

**Recommendation: keep Turkey (and the UAE) free.**
- No Stripe Checkout for TR/AE businesses in the starter phase. Founding businesses get Pro features free (the 12-month founding offer in `strategy.md`).
- **Why:**
  - Stripe forbids "persons located in, resident in, **or a citizen of**" Iran ([Stripe](https://stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses)).
  - A Turkish company whose owners are Iranian citizens, paying from a Turkish card, is exactly the case Stripe's own screening may flag, and **the whole German Stripe account is what's at stake**.
  - In the UAE, the 18 Aug 2026 trade halt makes Iran-linked payers worse still.
- **VAT while free:** nothing is supplied for money, so there is no VAT registration in Turkey or the UAE.
- **If paid later** (year 2, after the tax advisor, the sanctions lawyer and written confirmation from Stripe):

| | To registered businesses | To consumers or unregistered businesses |
|---|---|---|
| **Turkey** | No Turkish registration. The buyer pays the VAT itself under the reverse charge on its VAT return no. 2 ([Avalara](https://www.avalara.com/vatlive/en/country-guides/asia/turkey/turkish-vat-electronic-services.html), [Kintsugi](https://trykintsugi.com/sales-tax-guides/middle-east/turkey)). **Watch out:** Turkish payers must withhold **15% income tax on internet advertising services** paid to non-residents (Presidential Decision 476, from 2019; [KPMG Vergi](https://kpmgvergi.com/blog/internet-reklamlarina-yeni-15-stopaj-vergilemesi-nasil-hareket-etmeli/55)). Whether the Germany–Turkey tax treaty removes this is for the tax advisor. | Registration for Turkish VAT (20%) **from the first sale**, with monthly returns (round 5) |
| **UAE** | Reverse charge if the buyer has a TRN, the UAE VAT number ([Pinsent Masons](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/guides/gcc-vat-recent-developments-in-the-uae)) | Registration for 5% VAT **from the first dirham**. Businesses under AED 375k turnover often have no TRN and so count as consumers ([PayPro](https://payproglobal.com/saas-sales-tax/united-arab-emirates/)) |

- **On the German side:** services to non-EU businesses are taxed where the buyer is (§3a(2) UStG), so no German VAT. The invoice wording is for the tax advisor.
- **Rule if paid ever opens:** sell Pro only to businesses with a VKN (Turkey) or TRN (UAE), and only after sanctions screening.

### 2.3 Turkey

- **EİDS: confirmed, it covers only property and vehicle ads.**
  - It applies to "all property and vehicle ads given electronically", including Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp. In 2026, 82 agencies and dealers were fined TRY 100k each ([Alo Maliye, 24 Aug 2026](https://www.alomaliye.com/2026/08/24/eids-tum-elektronik-ilanlari-kapsiyor/), [Ministry of Trade guide](https://izmir.ticaret.gov.tr/data/6a8843dd269de1b0ac42f39a/EİDS%20Bilgilendirme%20Dokümanı_Ek_EİDS%20Nedir%20Nasıl%20Kullanılır.pdf)).
  - **A business profile of an agency, with no specific property, and event listings fall outside EİDS (my reading; for the lawyer).**
  - The grey zone: an agency page that shows "flats for rent from 25,000 TL" or photos of units is, in substance, a property ad. Agencies must link EİDS-verified ads on social media.
  - **That is why agencies and car dealers are excluded at the start.** If they're allowed later: only with their *Taşınmaz Ticareti Yetki Belgesi* (the estate-agent licence) number, no unit details, and at most a link to their EİDS-verified ads on sahibinden.
- **Law 5651:**
  - Event and business pages are content. Any page, or the whole domain, can be blocked by the BTK chairman's order without a court; 1.5M domains were blocked by the end of 2025 (round 5).
  - The likely triggers: political or opposition events (see the Jan 2026 ban), LGBTQ events, "insulting religion" (the reason given for the Namjoo cancellations), and gambling or crypto.
  - **Blocking peydoo.com in Turkey would also hit Turkish visitors to the Germany pages.** Use path-level hygiene: a `/turkey/...` take-down can be applied by us quickly.
- **KVKK:**
  - **Foreign data controllers must register in VERBIS regardless of size** (the size thresholds apply only to local controllers; [IBA](https://www.ibanet.org/Mandatory-data-protection-compliance-Turkey-VERBIS), [Gün + Partners](https://gun.av.tr/media/hr5lgjgs/the-exception-criteria-for-registration-obligation-with-the-data-controllers-registry-has-been-changed-by-the-decision-of-the-personal-data-protection-board.pdf)). They also have to appoint a Turkish representative ([Prighter](https://prighter.com/resources/turkey-data-protection-landscape-in-2025)).
  - **A free directory still processes personal data**: organiser and business-owner accounts, phones, IDs seen during verification, chats. Being free doesn't remove the trigger. A Turkey country page that targets people in Turkey is the strongest link.
  - Cost: about €1–3k a year for a representative (round 5 estimate), plus standard contracts for transfers abroad notified to the KVKK.
- **Rules that limit what a professional may publish:**
  - **Lawyers:** the TBB advertising rules (amended 9 Aug 2024) forbid lawyers from promoting themselves online "paid or free". Their own sites are limited to name, title, bar number, languages, address and contact ([Hukuki Haber](https://www.hukukihaber.net/yasal-duzenlemelerle-avukatlarin-reklam-yasagi/amp), [Resmî Gazete 9 Aug 2024](https://resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2024/08/20240809-1.htm)). A lawyer entry on Peydoo must stay a neutral listing with those fields only: no ratings, no "featured", no paid placement.
  - **Health:** the 2023 rules on promoting health services ban discounts, campaigns and gifts and claims that mislead, and they make whoever shares such a promotion jointly responsible ([Alo Maliye](https://www.alomaliye.com/2023/07/29/saglik-hizmetlerinde-tanitim-ve-bilgilendirme-faaliyetleri-hakkinda-yonetmelik/amp/)). Only clinics licensed by the Health Ministry, with information only; no hair-transplant brokers.
- **Event permits:** concerts need district or provincial permission and can be refused at short notice (Namjoo). We need a "cancelled by the authorities" status and copy for refunds, which go through the ticket seller.

### 2.4 UAE

- **Media law, Federal Decree-Law 55/2023:**
  - It defines "electronic media" as platforms that make "media **and advertising** content" available, and "advertisement" as marketing "for or without compensation" (Art. 1).
  - **Art. 12(1): the competent authority licenses "electronic and digital media activities" that provide news, promotion or advertising services, for or without compensation** ([law text](https://uae.shushin.io/uae-media-law/Federal-Decree-Law-55-2023), [official PDF](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2145/download)).
  - **Art. 2 says the law applies to activities "within the State".** Whether a German site targeting Dubai counts is open. Lexology reads the law as covering content delivered into the UAE (round 5).
  - **Fines:** AED 1k–1M, doubled to 2M for repeats (Cabinet Resolution 42/2025, [UAE legislation](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2868/download)).
  - **The content standards** (the Media Council's 20 standards, in effect since 29 May 2025, [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/uae-media-law-media-must-follow-20-key-standards-to-avoid-fines-of-up-to-dh1m-1.500157204)) require respect for Islam and the Abrahamic faiths and the state, and forbid "false news". Diaspora politics, war commentary and LGBTQ events would breach them.
  - **The regulator changed:** Federal Decree-Law 11/2025 created the **National Media Authority**, which replaced the UAE Media Council from 1 Jan 2026 ([WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/bn9xsue-uae-government-issues-federal-decree-law), [UAE legislation](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/3943/download)).
  - Individuals who advertise on social media need their own permit (Art. 12(2)), which is the influencer licence.
- **Health ads:** MOHAP issues a separate licence for a **"healthcare advertisement in a medical or commercial directory"**, and approved ads must show it ([MOHAP](https://mohap.gov.ae/assets/download/d0c91178/Infographic%20-%20Renewal%20of%20a%20License%20for%20a%20Healthcare%20Advertisement%20in%20a%20Medical%20or%20Commercial%20Directory%20-%20PDF%20362KB.pdf.aspx)). The DHA rules for Dubai were not checked. So clinics are out unless they show that licence.
- **Event permits:**
  - Ticketed events in Dubai need a DET e-Permit and a ticketing permit, and tickets are sold through licensed sellers ([DET](https://dubaidet.gov.ae/en/our-services/for-consumers-and-students/apply-to-sell-event-tickets), [Platinumlist](https://platinumlist.net/support/dubai-e-permit-application-service)).
  - Organisers need a trade licence and an organiser NOC (a no-objection certificate).
  - **I found no rule that ads must show the permit number, and none on platform liability.** But requiring the permit number and a link to a licensed seller is the obvious control.
- **The 2026 measures:**
  - Iranian residence permits were cancelled (March), later partly restored **[1 source]**;
  - Iranian schools, the hospital and the club were closed;
  - money changers were arrested;
  - a **full trade and financial halt with Iran since 18 Aug 2026**;
  - Iranian airlines are still suspended.
- **Other costs:** VAT for consumer sales from the first dirham, and the PDPL (the UAE data-protection law) may reach foreign controllers (not checked).

### 2.5 Safety

- **Track record:**
  - **Jamshid Sharmahd**, a German-Iranian, was abducted in Dubai in 2020 and executed in Oct 2024 ([ABC/AP](https://abcnews.go.com/International/wireStory/iranian-german-prisoner-jamshid-sharmahd-lived-us-executed-115225169)).
  - In Turkey: Masoud Molavi was shot in Istanbul (2019), Habib Chaab was abducted (2020), and a 17-person kidnap ring was arrested (2022) ([Iran Times](https://iran-times.com/turkey-arrests-17-for-plotting-to-kidnap-iranian-at-behest-of-tehran/?amp=1), [ecoi.net](https://www.ecoi.net/de/dokument/2045364.html)).
  - **Diaspora gatherings in both countries are watched.**
- **Rules for TR/AE event pages:**
  - **No political events, rallies, memorials with political framing or LGBTQ events on the TR/AE pages.** This protects users and the domain. It is a real trade-off against free expression, and the owner should decide it openly.
  - **The "sensitive event" defaults from round5-iran §3 apply to all parties**: the exact address, the organiser's personal name and photos of people are visible only to signed-in users with a verified phone.
  - Organisers appear as an organisation or brand, never as a private person's full name.
- **Verification data** (ID scans, registry extracts) is the most dangerous thing we'd hold. Look at it during a video call and **don't store copies**. Keep only "checked on {date} by {staff}, document type, registry number".

## 3. Design

### 3.1 The Turkey country page `/turkey` (later `/uae`)

- **Header:** «ترکیه: رویدادها و کسب‌وکارهای تأییدشده» ("Turkey: events and verified businesses"), with one line saying why there are no person-to-person ads: "Turkish law requires e-Devlet verification for property and car ads; we keep Turkey to events and verified businesses".
- **Blocks:**
  1. **Events** (the existing `/{country}/events` with city chips Istanbul, Ankara, Antalya, İzmir, Van; "this weekend"; "Nowruz / Yalda" collections);
  2. **Verified businesses** by category (a directory of `/b/{slug}` pages, each with the badge «تأییدشده» and the date of the last check);

### 3.2 Allowed business categories (starter)

### 3.3 Verification

**Turkey business, done by the owner in the starter phase. About 20–40 minutes each (my estimate), and yearly.**
1. **Tax plate** (*vergi levhası*) PDF with barcode, checked on e-Devlet's *Barkodlu Belge Doğrulama* ([e-Devlet guide](https://www.e-yasamrehberi.com/e-devlet/e-devlet-belge-dogrulama.html)). Record the tax number and tax office.
2. **For companies:** the **MERSİS number** and the latest **Türkiye Ticaret Sicili Gazetesi** notice (registry gazette) showing partners and managers. **Every partner and manager is screened** (§2.1).
3. **Professionals:** bar or chamber number, the notary's sworn-translator record, the Health Ministry licence for clinics.
4. **Proof of place:** a photo of the shop front or a map listing, plus a callback to the public phone number.
5. **A short video call with the owner or manager** in Persian; ID shown, not stored.
6. **Sanctions screening** pass. Then `scripts/verify-business.ts <slug> verified` writes `audit_log`.
7. Store the **expiry or next-check date**; the badge lapses by itself.

**UAE business, if it ever opens:**
- the **trade licence number**, checked on the federal **National Economic Register** ([u.ae](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/business/important-digital-services/national-economic-register)) or the emirate or free-zone register (DET, DMCC and others);
- the licence's activity must match the category;
- partners and managers on the licence are screened;
- the TRN (if any) is checked on the FTA site;
- the MOHAP or DHA ad licence for clinics;
- the licence expiry date is stored.

**Event organisers (both countries):**
- either a verified business, a registered association, or a venue;
- for the first event: confirmation through the venue's **public** contact details;
- in Dubai: the **DET permit number** is a required field, and the ticket link must go to a licensed seller;
- the existing limits apply: a verified phone (+90 / +971; +98 is refused), 2–5 events a day, https only, no URL shorteners.

**Who checks:**
- the owner at first, with a cap of about **50 businesses and 10 organisers in the starter** (my estimate of what one person can keep up with, including Turkish documents);
- a paid Turkish-speaking freelancer or a vetted association later. The partner risk from round 5 still applies: vet their politics and any regime links, and our liability stays.

## 6. Lawyer and tax-advisor questions

**Turkey**
1. Does KVKK apply to a German site with a Turkey page and Turkish organiser and business accounts? Must we register in VERBIS and appoint a representative? Which transfer tool (standard contracts) works for EU servers and US processors?
2. Confirm that EİDS doesn't cover agency *profiles* without unit details, or event listings. Does EİDS bind a foreign platform at all?
3. Law 5651: our duties as a content and hosting provider; how to receive and answer BTK or court notices; whether a block can be limited to a path.
4. Can a third-party directory list lawyers under the TBB rules (2024), and clinics under the 2023 health-promotion rules, without making us or them liable? Which fields are allowed?
5. Do we carry any liability for a concert listed on Peydoo that the authorities later ban?
6. Tax: if Pro is sold later, does the 15% withholding on internet advertising (Decision 476) apply to payments to us, and does the Germany–Turkey treaty remove it? Is the reverse charge enough with no Turkish registration?

7. Does Decree-Law 55/2023 Art. 12(1) (with Art. 2 "within the State") require a foreign, free Persian events and business directory that targets Dubai to hold a licence from the National Media Authority? What does that cost, and what if it isn't held?
8. Are we liable for an event without a DET permit, or a clinic without a MOHAP or DHA ad licence, listed by a user?
9. Does the UAE PDPL reach us? And does the 18 Aug 2026 trade halt affect a German platform serving Iranian residents of the UAE?

**Sanctions and Stripe (both)**

10. Is a free business page (with free Pro features) "economic resources" under Reg. 267/2012 or 2023/1529? Is our screening method (OpenSanctions API, monthly re-checks, the registry extract for the 50% rule) enough for a sole trader?
11. Is the forbidden-category list in §2.1 complete? Is "company formation" correctly forbidden?
12. Stripe: does "citizen of Iran" reach a Turkish or UAE company with Iranian-citizen owners? Ask Stripe in writing before any TR/AE sale.
