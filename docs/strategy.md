# Launch strategy

Oct 22, 2026 · summary of the full study, "Peydoo launch strategy": https://claude.ai/artifact/CRYapwgCPUn9duJiKZWk14 (a Claude Doc with sources; private until the owner shares it)

**In one line:** win Berlin first, then ride Telegram outward. Launch deep in one country and one city, use Telegram channels as distribution, and seed real supply by hand with consent. No fake listings.

## Five recommendations

1. **Deep, not wide.** Germany and Austria stay open, but hands-on seeding happens in Berlin only. Then Hamburg, Cologne–Bonn–Düsseldorf and Vienna, each after the previous city passes the gates below. Canada (Toronto) is the second country, around month 9–12; the US after it. Turkey, the UK and the UAE stay closed in year one. Claim the brand elsewhere with an EU trademark (about €850) and Madrid designations (about CHF 2,250 plus the basic fee for US, CA, UK, AU, TR in two classes), domains and handles, not with empty country pages.
2. **Be the structured layer behind Telegram, not its rival.** The n8n posting becomes a partner programme: channel admins who invite the bot get a free city feed and the weekly events digest, with a referral deep link. Start with the @berlin_online family (3,886 subscribers, takes ads through a bot). Every listing gets a ready-made Telegram post. Never post where the bot was not invited.
3. **No fake listings, users or counters.** Very likely unlawful (UWG Annex Nos. 22, 23b/c, 5; §§ 5, 5a UWG; LG Berlin 16 O 62/21 on a dating site's own fake profiles; DSA Art. 25), fraud exposure once Pro and bumps are sold, and fatal to trust if discovered. Copying listings from Kleinanzeigen or Telegram is not a substitute (§ 87b and § 72 UrhG, GDPR). Seed instead with the 20–50 founding businesses on free Pro, labelled concierge posting for real sellers who agree, real events with organisers' consent, the founder's own items and honest empty states.
4. **Lead with what Telegram and Kleinanzeigen serve worst:** rooms with an «Anmeldung möglich» field and a deposit-scam banner, Persian-speaking services and businesses, newcomer jobs, and events as the weekly traffic engine. Ban havaleh and money exchange (§ 63 ZAG) and Iran-bound parcel carrying (EU sanctions reimposed 29 Sep 2025) from day one.
5. **The admin and moderation page is the launch blocker (DSA Art. 16 and 17); AI posting is not.** If AI posting would push launch past Yalda (21 Dec), launch first and ship it within 60 days.

## Gates (proposals, not benchmarks)

- **Next city**, held for 4 weeks: ≥100 live listings in housing, services and jobs; ≥50% of new listings get a chat within 7 days; ≥30 weekly posters.
- **Next country**, additionally: gates met in two German cities; moderation queue cleared within 24 h on business days for 6 weeks with under 5 h a week; server under ~70% memory at peak or a second server budgeted; one local partner channel or organisation; the country checklist in `docs/setup.md` done; a few hundred waitlist sign-ups from the main city.

## Weekly metrics per city

Active listings, weekly posters, chats started (and the share of new listings with a chat within 7 days), returning visitors (two separate days within 28 days). Also partner-channel clicks and moderation queue age.

## Product items this implies

- Before launch: banned terms («حواله», «صرافی», «تبدیل ارز», «انتقال پول», «مسافربر», «پیک مسافری», «فروش وقت سفارت», export to Iran) with Persian explanations; housing fields and scam banner; geoblock Iran and refuse +98 numbers in the bot; privacy policy names the transfer to Telegram; keep "Iran" out of Stripe statement descriptors; a SEPA or Mollie fallback.
- After launch, in order: share-to-Telegram post and partner feeds, AI posting and moderation, PWA push, jobs fields, inline bot, directory of Persian-speaking professionals on Pro pages, newcomer guides, Telegram Mini App, optional ID badge. Native apps and escrow later.

## Decisions for the owner

Launch date (before Yalda without AI posting, or later with it); first seeding city (Berlin as planned, or Hamburg, which is as large); free Pro length for founding businesses (12 months suggested); offering the @berlin_online family a partnership first; filing the EU trademark before launch.
